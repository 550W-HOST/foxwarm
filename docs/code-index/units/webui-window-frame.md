## Purpose

Puts the WebUI into the window's title area for three frame providers: the Windows Desktop host, the macOS Desktop host, and an installed Chromium PWA in window-controls overlay. The rows that already sit at the top of the desktop layout become one title row; there is no separate title bar. Ordinary browser tabs, Safari, Firefox and mobile or standalone PWAs have no provider and render exactly as before.

## Contract

### D-window-frame

[2026-11-13] User decisions for the custom Desktop/PWA window frame.

- **Model.** `html[data-foxwarm-frame="windows"|"macos"|"overlay"]` plus `--foxwarm-frame-left|right|top` (CSS px) describe the window-control zones at the top corners. Layout reads only these. `html[data-foxwarm-window~="inactive|maximized|fullscreen"]` mirrors the host's window state; nothing is styled with it (no inactive-window styling).
- **Desktop hosts → page.** At document creation, for the top-level document of the owned origin only, the host defines `window.foxwarmFrame = { platform, left, right, top, state }`. On a change it replaces the object and dispatches `foxwarm-frame` on `window`.
- **Page → Desktop hosts.** One one-way message, `{ type: "frame-regions", regions: [[x, y, w, h], …], colorScheme: "light" | "dark" }`, at most 128 rectangles in viewport CSS px, sent only when a host channel exists, at most once per animation frame and only when it changes. Windows: `IsWebMessageEnabled` with `WebMessageReceived`, accepting only messages whose source is the owned origin. macOS: one `WKScriptMessageHandler` named `foxwarmFrame`, main frame and owned origin only. Both hosts reject anything that does not match the exact schema (`DesktopFrameRegions.Parse`). There are no host objects, no replies and no other capabilities. This is the only exception to the hosts' no-bridge contracts ([Windows](./host-runtime.md#desktop-boundary), [macOS](./desktop-macos.md#native-shell-and-security)).
- **PWA.** `manifest.json` declares `display_override: ["window-controls-overlay"]` with `display: "standalone"` as fallback. Under `(display-mode: window-controls-overlay)` the module sets `data-foxwarm-frame="overlay"` and `index.css` derives the insets from `env(titlebar-area-*)`; drag surfaces are CSS `app-region: drag`. Switching the overlay off removes the attribute and every frame rule. The browser paints the overlay controls on `theme-color`, so while the overlay is on the live `meta[name=theme-color]` is the surface under the controls (`frameSurfaceColor`: the topmost page element at the middle of the larger control zone, composited over its ancestors' backgrounds). It is re-read on every theme change and frame layout, and when the boot screen, which covers the controls during the first layouts, is removed. The `foxwarm_canvas` cookie, and with it the manifest's `theme_color` and `background_color`, stays the canvas, which is also the launch background (`setFrameChromeColor` in `browserChrome.ts`).
- **Title rows.** Rows opt in with `data-frame-row`: `column` for the sidebar and the collapsed rail (only marked in the band; their title-row segment is laid out by CSS), `inline` for a pane's tab strip (it reserves the overlap plus a 40 px drag gap). A row that doesn't intersect a control zone is unchanged. In split layouts only the pane whose row intersects a zone reserves anything.
- **Narrow rows.** [2026-11-13] User decision. A row that intersects a zone first lets its tabs overflow-scroll. When the width left beside the reservation is less than one tab at its minimum width plus the row's own controls and padding (the row marks its tab list `data-frame-row-fill`; everything else in the row counts as fixed), the row keeps its full width below a window-drag band as tall as the controls, above that pane only (`data-frame-banded`). A row without a fill element never bands. No strip action stays under the controls or the drag gap. Top/bottom splits keep the top pane's full width, so they normally don't band.
- **Framed layout.** [2026-11-14] User decision (direction C, "chrome sidebar and content islands"), replacing the 2026-11-13 Windows top-row treatment (flush corner pane, painted gutters) on every provider. Desktop layouts only; the narrow mobile shell is unchanged.
  - The window background (`canvas-edge`) is chrome. The title row, the sidebar and the status row sit on it; only workbench panes are cards (bordered, 10 px radius), with 8 px gutters between them and at the right edge, and the cards end at the status row.
  - One title row spans the window, as tall as the window controls and at least 36 px (Windows 48 px at 100 %, macOS 38 px, the PWA overlay's reported height). The sidebar's brand row is its left end; the collapsed rail's expand toggle is the rail's title row. Each top pane's tab strip is its segment over that pane: tabs sit on the pane's card, and every title-row control is centred on the row.
  - Title-row buttons (the Setup split button and sidebar toggle in the brand row, the rail toggle, the strip's own buttons) are borderless, with a hover fill only. They are named by data attribute or class (`data-global-ui-settings-split`, `data-sidebar-toggle`, `.foxwarm-sidebar-rail-toggle`, direct children of `.foxwarm-workbench-tab-toolbar`), never by descendant or structural selectors, so floating menus and popovers inside the row keep their own surfaces. Inactive tabs are text on the chrome with thin separators; the active tab takes the header surface, bordered on three sides, and joins its card.
  - Under a frame every pane keeps its tab strip, a sole tab included, so the title row is the same in every layout (`windowFrameActive` / `subscribeWindowFrame`; switching the frame off restores the sole-tab content header).
  - Controls at the top left (macOS lights, `data-foxwarm-frame-leading`): the brand row starts after them and shows the logo only, as the app name is in the menu bar; the rail toggle sits right after them. Full screen (no zones) shows the wordmark.
  - A narrow pane that bands (above) leaves its title-row segment as blank drag chrome and moves its strip into the top of its card, the strip's top border aligned with the other cards' top edge.
  - Lower panes of a top/bottom split keep a 40 px strip on the chrome above their card.
  - [2026-11-14] Popup leaves ([app windows](./host-runtime.md#d-desktop-app-windows) on Desktop) have one title row on the chrome: the logo and the popup's title (the `document.title` subject), clear of the control zones, the whole row a drag surface (`data-frame-row="column"`). The leaf's content is one card below it with 8 px gutters. Narrow popups keep this row: the mobile strip stays a transparent drag surface over it and the content gets no extra top inset. Without a provider popups are unchanged.
- **Edges.** A drag surface (the drag band of a narrow row included) near the window's top, left or right edge (within 24 px of shell gutter, panel border and padding) is extended to that edge by fixed fill elements, provided the extension covers only chrome: no text, media or interactive element. Operating-system resize borders stay with the system.
- **Narrow windows** (< 768 px) keep the mobile shell. A canvas-coloured drag strip of `--foxwarm-frame-top` height sits above it, and `--foxwarm-safe-area-top` is raised by the same amount.
- **Accessibility.** Spacers and fills are `aria-hidden` and add no focus stops. Under `forced-colors: active` the strip paints `Canvas`. Keyboard window control stays native.

### Hosts

- Windows ([host](./host-runtime.md#desktop-boundary)): `ExtendsContentIntoTitleBar` with `PreferredHeightOption.Tall` (48 px, 144 px of caption buttons at 100 %; the page never assumes these numbers). Insets are `TitleBar.LeftInset/RightInset/Height` divided by the XAML rasterization scale; updates follow `AppWindow.Changed`, `XamlRoot.Changed` and activation. Every report sets explicit `InputNonClientPointerSource` caption rectangles, scaled and clipped against the caption-button zone, and an empty list when there are none, because WinUI's default caption band swallows clicks and the WinUI WebView2 control ignores `app-region`. Caption button colours follow `colorScheme` with transparent backgrounds, except under high contrast. `Window.Title` follows `document.title`.
- macOS ([host](./desktop-macos.md#native-shell-and-security)): `fullSizeContentView`, a transparent title bar with a hidden title, and an empty toolbar in the compact unified style for the 38 px title area. `left` is the zoom button's right edge plus 12 px; in full screen the toolbar is hidden and all insets are 0. A transparent view above the web view takes a press only inside a reported rectangle and never over the lights: mouse-down calls `performWindowDrag`, a double-click follows `AppleActionOnDoubleClick`, and wheel and secondary clicks are forwarded to the page. `colorScheme` sets `window.appearance`; `NSWindow.title` follows `document.title` for accessibility and the Window menu.

## Function index

- `initializeWindowFrame()` — called once from `main.tsx`; detects the provider and starts or stops the frame runtime. Without a provider it installs only the `foxwarm-frame` and overlay media listeners.
- `readHostFrame(value)` — validates `window.foxwarmFrame`.
- `frameRowLayout(row, zones, kind, minContent)` — in-band flag, padding and band decision for one row.
- `windowFrameActive()`, `subscribeWindowFrame(listener)` — whether a frame provider is active, as a React external store.
- `frameEdgeFillCandidates(surface, width)`, `computeFrameEdgeFills(document)` — edge extensions and their chrome-only check by hit testing.
- `collectFrameDragRects(document)` — the rectangles reported to the hosts.
- `FrameEdges` — drag spacers at both ends of an inline row.
- `frameSurfaceColor(document, zones)` — overlay `theme-color` source.
- `DesktopFrameRegions.Parse/Clip` — host-side schema validation and device-pixel clipping, shared by both hosts.

## Tests

- `windowFrame.test.mjs`: host-state validation, rectangle subtraction, row avoidance for Windows and macOS geometry, the narrow-row band threshold, edge-fill candidates.
- `windowFrameRects.e2e.mjs`: fixture DOMs in Chromium; regions exclude interactive descendants, floating layers and out-of-band rows; edge fills extend through gutter and padding but never across controls or text; the overlay surface colour composites translucent layers and ignores frame fills.
- `windowFrame.e2e.mjs`: the demo build with an injected `window.foxwarmFrame` and a recording host channel (popup leaves on both hosts, wide and narrow: title row height, title clear of the controls, whole row draggable, card gutters; unchanged without a provider; no reported rectangle covers a visible control, no control under the window controls, brand row, rail title row and gap reach the window edges). Framed layout on Windows, light and dark, expanded and collapsed: chrome background, transparent sidebar and status row, one title row exactly as tall as the caption buttons with the brand, tabs and controls centred on it, borderless title-row buttons, inactive tabs without a slab, the active tab on the header surface, cards below the title row ending at the status row with an 8 px right gutter. A sole tab keeps its strip only under a frame. The opened Setup menu is opaque under the frame, light and dark, and its panel and controls compute the same as in a browser without a provider. Row splits at 50, 40 and 25 %: only the corner pane reserves, 8 px between cards, and the narrow ones band with a draggable blank title row and their strip at the card top aligned with the other cards; a top/bottom split keeps a 40 px lower strip; a standard-height caption keeps the 36 px minimum. macOS: logo-only brand and the rail toggle after the lights, the lights band drags, full screen reports nothing and shows the wordmark. Split panes keep pane drag, menus stay clickable, 700 px strip and 800 px desktop layout. A stubbed overlay media query and inset variables for the PWA (headless Chromium emulates neither): `theme-color` is the chrome under the controls once the boot screen is gone and follows a theme change while the cookie keeps the canvas; switching the overlay off renders the same pixels as a page without a provider (allowing only anti-aliasing noise). An unchanged page without a provider.
- `documentOverflow.e2e.mjs` ([D-webui-app-shell-height](./webui-app.md#d-webui-app-shell-height)): the frame layout adds no document scroll range at common sizes, display scales and fractional-viewport windows.
- `browserChrome.e2e.mjs`: the frame colour overrides only the live `theme-color`; clearing it restores the canvas.
- `DesktopFrameRegionsTests.cs`: message schema and limits, clipping.
